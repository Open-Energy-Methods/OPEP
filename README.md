# Open Physical Engineering Programme (OPEP)

Development of a compact, reconfigurable experimental platform for multidisciplinary engineering investigations.

This is an independent engineering hobby project undertaken in my spare time. There is no fixed timeline or completion deadline. The intention is to develop shared mechanical interfaces and measurement infrastructure for small-scale experiments and to compare physical measurements with analytical and numerical predictions.

## Initial scope

The initial work focuses on electronic component heating and cooling, electrical and thermal instrumentation, measurement uncertainty, precision fixture manufacturing, prototype inspection, assembly repeatability, numerical verification and independent experimental validation.

Structural mechanics, fluid mechanics, turbomachinery and gas-turbine-related modelling, controls, vibration, acoustics, environmental and reliability studies, diagnostics and digital twins are possible future extensions, not current deliverables. Actual gas turbine operation and combustion testing are outside the current scope.

The scope is preliminary. Investigations depend on access to suitable equipment, instruments, materials, funding, technical expertise and workshop permissions. Where essential resources cannot reasonably be secured, the method may be modified, or the investigation deferred or excluded. The reasons will be documented.

Practical work is intended to take place at the KTH EECS Student Workshop where permitted. This is a personal project, not a KTH-sponsored or institutionally approved research programme.

## Method

The emphasis is on measurement uncertainty, boundary conditions, independent repetitions, and quantitative comparison between measurements and models. Numerical verification is distinguished from experimental validation. No physical results are reported at this stage, and no claim of research novelty is made.

The existing [Electronics Bench Testing project](https://github.com/Open-Energy-Methods/electronics-bench-testing) is a related specialist workstream with preliminary design and synthetic modelling work.

## Technical feedback

Suggestions for feasible experiments, industrial testing methods, instrumentation, relevant literature and improvements to the methodology are welcome. Please use the [issue tracker](https://github.com/Open-Energy-Methods/OPEP/issues) or see [contribution guidance](CONTRIBUTING.md).

Practical contributions to designs, experiments, models, software and documentation are also welcome. Please discuss the proposed work and obtain the maintainer's agreement in an issue before developing changes intended for OPEP. Agreed changes can be submitted as pull requests for review; inclusion in the project is at the maintainer's discretion.

The intention is to make original engineering designs, calculations, procedures, software and resulting datasets freely accessible, where licensing, safety and facility permissions permit. Applicable licences will be specified for released materials. Third-party material is not automatically covered.
