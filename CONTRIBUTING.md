# Technical feedback

OPEP is a spare-time engineering project with no fixed schedule. Feedback is welcome from industry practitioners, researchers, academics and other interested engineers.

Please open an issue with a description of the proposed experiment or correction. Where relevant, include:

- The engineering question and its practical or scientific relevance.
- A suggested method, measurable quantities and suitable reference measurements.
- Required equipment, approximate resource needs and any safety constraints.
- References to relevant publications, standards or industrial practice, if available.
- Known limitations or reasons why the method may not be feasible.

Short suggestions are also welcome. There is no requirement to provide a complete experimental design.

Suggestions will be assessed for scientific relevance, measurement quality, resource availability and safety. Investigations may be retained, modified, deferred or excluded. Acceptance of a suggestion does not imply a commitment to implement it.

Please do not share confidential information, restricted manuals, unpublished third-party data or material you are not entitled to disclose. Contributors may be acknowledged with their consent; any substantial research collaboration or authorship arrangements will be discussed separately.

No proposed experiment is an approved operating procedure. Actual fabrication and testing remain subject to local permissions and competent review.
